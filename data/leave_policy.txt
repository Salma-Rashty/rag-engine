Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 14 days of paid annual leave per year. Annual leave is earned after completing one full year of continuous employment. Employees who have not yet completed one year may request unpaid leave, subject to manager approval.

Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Unused annual leave can be carried over to the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave beyond that limit is forfeited at the end of the calendar year.

In addition to annual leave, employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave requires a medical certificate if it exceeds two consecutive days.
