Meals and Transportation

The company cafeteria serves lunch every weekday between 12.00 and 13.00. Meals are subsidized, and employees only pay a small fixed amount per meal. Vegetarian and vegan options are available daily, and the weekly menu is posted every Monday.

Free coffee and tea are available throughout the day in the kitchen areas on every floor. Snacks are restocked twice a week.

A company shuttle service is available for commuting. The morning shuttle arrives at the office at 08.30, and the evening shuttle leaves from the main entrance at 18.00. Shuttle routes and pickup points are listed on the internal transport page.
